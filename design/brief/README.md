# Design brief

`DESIGN_BRIEF.md` is the brief Ryan wrote before the 2.0 redesign: three reference apps and what to take from each, what to keep from v1, what was wrong with it, how the app is actually used, and what the recipe view had to feel like.

It fed the three directions in [`../mockups/`](../mockups/). Ryan tested those on his phone and chose one; the result is written up in [`../SYSTEM.md`](../SYSTEM.md), and the reasoning behind each choice is in [`../../docs/DECISIONS.md`](../../docs/DECISIONS.md).
