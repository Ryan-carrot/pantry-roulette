# Roadmap

## Shipped in 2.0

- A new look: a darker, warmer palette, sharper type, and a layout built for a phone on the kitchen counter.
- A real spin: a reel runs through the recipes it found and lands on yours.
- A vibe dial you swipe instead of a dropdown.
- A recipe view you can cook from: tick ingredients off as you add them, large numbered steps, and Save, Print and Close always within reach.
- A rough cook-time estimate, read from the recipe's own instructions.
- No repeats: recipes you've already seen are skipped until you clear your history.
- "Surprise me", for when you don't want to type anything.
- Faster repeat spins, clearer error messages, and better keyboard and screen-reader support.

## Next

- **Servings scaling.** Adjust a recipe up or down and have the quantities follow.
- **A real logo.**
- **A feedback form**, so you can tell us what works and what doesn't without opening your email.

## Not planned

- **Accounts.** Your saved recipes stay on your device.
- **Payments.** This is a free utility.
